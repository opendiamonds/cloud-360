### Summary

The artifact is well-grounded on almost every checkable fact against the real `deploy/` and `.github/workflows/` files — I could not find a single misquoted path, line number, or job name. The problems are in the design's own internal reasoning rather than in its citations: the restart-recovery verification (cicd-pipeline.md §三) gates on a healthcheck that this unit's own approved `reliability-design.md` explicitly says must not be trusted for availability, which undermines the one real test this unit has for its heaviest NFR (R-01, Critical); the "no additional session loss" mitigation for that same restart is true only for the specific deploy that also changes Redis's compose config, not for the routine deploys the check will actually run on most of the time (R-02, Major); and `traceability.json` silently omits one upstream id (`NFR8.42`) that the stage actually discharged, making the stage's self-reported coverage count technically wrong by one even though no sensor catches it (R-03, Minor).
