### Summary

The functional design for `U19 membership-service` is unusually thorough in its self-auditing (accurate `user_router.py` line-number re-verification, a fully reconciled 29-rule/20-AC traceability matrix, honest disclosure of several residual gaps) and its reasoning on `[FD1]`/`[FD2]`/`[FD3]` is sound. But it rests on at least one load-bearing claim — that `reselection_events.project_id` has no foreign key to `projects.id` — that directly contradicts the one shared contract (`K-15`) available to check it against, and it does not disclose that the "last admin" invariant it is built to protect is already false for every pre-existing project the day this unit ships, because no backfill of `project_members` for existing projects is specified anywhere upstream or in this unit. Those two findings, plus the unverified claim that the three "common upstream" guard paths genuinely share one implementation, are blocking; the AC12.3.3 traceability gap and the uncorroborated "19 of 20" figure are secondary but real documentation defects that would mislead an implementer or a later auditor.
