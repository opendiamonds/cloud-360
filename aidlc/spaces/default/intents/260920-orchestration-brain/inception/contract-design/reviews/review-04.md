### Summary

The three revision-2 fixes landed and are correct, not merely present: R-07's new `set_work_target`/`work_target` pair rides `U14 → U13 → U10 → U7`, every hop of which is in the approved 25-edge DAG, and `U16` reaches the write legitimately through the `onObjectSelected` prop. Counts, sensors and all 19 YAML blocks survived the revision. What the revision did not resolve is the class of defect R-01 and R-07 belong to: a declared capability with no transport. `set_sharing_mode` is the third instance (R-10), and it sits in the second half of the very `components.md` sentence the lead quoted to justify the first fix. Routing persistence through the socket also leaves `[RA:FR2.2]` without a mechanism wherever no socket exists — no contract says `/workspace` and `/assessment` connect, and there is no HTTP read of the current work target (R-12) — and `session_key` itself still has no producer anywhere in the seventeen contracts (R-11). Counts by class: `new-introduced-by-revision-2` 1 (R-12, a consequence of choosing the WS route), `pre-existing` 3 (R-10, R-11, R-13), `resolved-this-round` 3 (R-07, R-08, R-09). I do not consider the artifact ready for the gate: three Majors remain, and R-10 and R-12 each leave a Must-level requirement with no defined mechanism — but all three are narrow, locatable edits to `K-02`, `K-12` and `K-07`, not a redesign.
