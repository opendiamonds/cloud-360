### Summary

Defect origin this round: **new-introduced-by-revision-3 1** (R-16, Minor — the fan-out mechanism is new in this revision), **pre-existing 2** (R-14 and R-15, both present since revision 1 and both missed by three passes of sweeping), **resolved-this-round 4** (R-10, R-11, R-12, R-13 — all four verified as correct, not merely present). The 1-to-3 ratio of the earlier rounds has not reversed, but its character has: revision 3 introduced almost nothing, and what remains is genuinely pre-existing.

The artifact is **not ready for the gate**, and the reason is a fourth instance of the class that caused the three prior rejections — found on the axis the sweep did not run: `MemoryFacade.write` is declared with no caller anywhere in the seventeen contracts, and the same `components.md` half-sentence trap applies (「三種記憶的讀寫」, read half only). With no writer, the memory capability and four units are inert and four Must requirements have no mechanism, and the assignment cannot be inferred here because the plausible writer has no edge to `U8`. R-15 is adjacent and narrow: the single-key model is right, but `[DD:E6]`'s 「兩段」 cannot be expressed by one `messageHistory` field and an `append_message` with no segment parameter, so FR3.2 and FR3.3 cannot both hold as written — and revision 3's "satisfied by construction" claim overstates what is closed. Both are locatable edits to `K-08` and `K-09` plus one open-question row, not a redesign; R-16 is a one-line disclosure.
