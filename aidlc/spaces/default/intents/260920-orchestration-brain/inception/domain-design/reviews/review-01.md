### Summary

The artifact is unusually well self-audited — its own "契約端點三問" and "送審前自檢" sections catch real gaps (DG-1/DG-2/DG-3, the E8 reframing, the OQ-1 non-decision) before a reviewer has to, and every checkable number, cross-reference, and stage-graph claim I re-derived independently came back correct. The three findings above are things the self-audit did not catch: an unresolved same-process authorization-bypass shape around `ProjectHierarchy` that mirrors a documented pre-existing repo vulnerability class, a tech-stack selection (pgvector/Ollama/bge-m3/fastembed) that the stage's own charter says belongs to a later stage and that the artifact treats inconsistently relative to how it handled `IntentRouter`'s model choice, and a missing ADR-0006 four-dimension security table that `project.md` mandates for changes touching IAM/network-exposure/audit-logging (all three of which this design does touch). All three are Major, none Critical — none breaks a passed contract, and R-01/R-03 are gaps of omission rather than contradictions with already-approved upstream artifacts. By the review rubric's mechanical rule (NOT-READY if any Critical OR >2 Major), three Majors crosses the threshold, so the verdict below is NOT-READY. This is an advisory-class pass with no fix-and-re-review loop behind it, so this verdict informs the human at the gate rather than blocking progress on its own — none of the three findings makes the six-component decomposition itself unbuildable, and a developer could proceed from this artifact on everything except the `ProjectHierarchy` authorization-path ambiguity (R-01), which is the one finding I'd weigh most heavily before approving as-is.
