### Summary

The mechanical floor (sensors, id-grammar fix, traceability wiring, story/AC
counts, the well-evidenced US4.3/N-8 addition) is solid, and several of the
mob's Critical findings (AC1.4.3/AC1.4.4 rewrite, AC9.1.4 addition, FR4.4/
FR10.3/FR8.3/FR9.4 landing) were integrated carefully and are individually
excellent work. But the core value of running `mode: mob` is the three
independent specialist passes, and a systematic comparison of all three
contribution files against the integrated `stories.md` shows roughly a dozen
concrete, evidence-backed findings — several explicitly rated Major by their
authors, several targeting exactly the "unreachable Given" / "unfalsifiable
Then" defect class this stage claims zero tolerance for and demonstrably
fixed elsewhere — were silently dropped with no disposition recorded in
Assumptions or any DoD section. One (`AC4.3.1`) was left with a dangling
"see the note below" pointer to a note that doesn't exist. This is well past
the ≤2-Major threshold for READY, and the pattern (selective integration of
the most narratively prominent findings, silent loss of the rest) is itself
worth flagging to whoever re-runs integration: the fix is not a rewrite of
the good work already done, it's closing the loop on findings that were
already paid for and already sitting in the contribution files.
