### Summary

The DAG mathematics, kind distribution, story-count arithmetic, and the carry-forward of domain-design's accepted risk `R-01` are all independently verified correct — this is a well self-audited artifact by the numbers. However, the stage drops three classes of upstream-disclosed risk that it explicitly set a precedent for disclosing elsewhere (R-01/R-03 carry-forward): domain-design's `DG-1`/`DG-2`/`DG-3` contract gaps (one of which, `DG-2`, threatens an invariant this stage's own `U4` notes cite as settled), and the `OQ-4`/`OQ-10` no-fallback blocker on `U11` that is structurally identical to the `OQ-13`/`U9` blocker the stage did disclose. Separately, one story-to-unit assignment (`US3.1`) names a unit (`U16`) that does not own the UI control the story's AC requires, and the stage inherits domain-design's ADR-0006 four-dimension-table gap (review finding `R-03`) without adding one itself despite this stage's own units touching three of the four dimensions. Four Major findings exceed the review rubric's threshold (NOT-READY if any Critical OR >2 Major); none is Critical, and all are omissions rather than structural DAG defects, so a developer could largely proceed, but the gate reviewer/human should see these before approving.
