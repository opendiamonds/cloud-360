### Per-attack-point disposition

1. **Capability 11 bundling.** Checked directly against `scope-document.md` (revision 2) and `[R10]`. The capability-level bundle is real (five listed responsibilities) and `[R10]=B` is a genuine page/tab seam, not a rename of the same problem — I could not construct a counter-example where the two-page split fails to separate concerns at the *navigation* level. But the split does not resolve the *verification* level, which is a distinct question this stage is not required to answer — see R-03 for what I found and why I flagged it Major rather than Critical (it's a downstream risk to flag, not a defect in this stage's own deliverable).

2. **`[R12]=D` fixed per-agent field groups.** Checked the stated rationale ("free-form key-value hands semantics to an LLM, and this repo has a structural blind spot on all LLM paths") against `team.md`'s recorded blind-spot language — it matches verbatim, this is not a fabricated justification. What breaks first when an agent needs an unanticipated field: by the answer's own text, "新 agent 上線要同步加一組定義" — i.e., the cost is a coordinated release (new agent ships with its field-group definition), not silent data loss or a runtime failure. That is an honest, named cost, not a hidden one. I did not find this to be a rationalization; the free-form alternative genuinely would reopen the repo's named LLM blind spot with no mitigation offered in its place. No finding.

3. **Figma two-class judgment (§19).** Checked. The criterion itself ("is this element the screen-carrier of an already-approved AC") is defensible and was applied consistently to `G-1`/`G-2` with correctly cited concrete sections. It was **not** carried through for `G-3`/`G-4` — see R-02. This is the one place I'd push back hardest: the criterion is fine, its execution is half-done, and half-done in the direction that happens to require no new drawing.

4. **Flow 7 as a dotted/optional path.** Checked against `scope-document.md`'s revision-2 "本站不主張的一項" and against Flow 2's mechanics. Capability 10's definition ("辨識成本需求、路由、轉譯狀態事件") genuinely does not require any capability-11 field to produce an answer — Flow 2 step 2 calls `/api/cost/v1` directly with no field lookup in between. I did not find a real hidden dependency; the "one answer now, a better answer once fields exist" framing matches what Flow 7 draws. No finding.

5. **Testability.** §16's "未填需求欄位 4" is a count of a real, well-defined set (fields marked "未填" in §17), so it is a checkable pass/fail number once the field list exists — testable. §18's read-only memory-summary tab is testable at the presentation contract level (no editable controls, no save affordance, the disclaimer text is present) but the underlying pipeline that produces the summary content is explicitly and correctly deferred ("四個問題本站一個都不替它假設") — that's an honest scope boundary, not an untestable claim, since the AC that would exist at this stage is about the screen being read-only, not about summary correctness. No finding beyond what's already in R-03 about the tab split.

6. **What's missing that a user will immediately need.** Found R-01 (member management button with no behind-it screen, tied to genuinely new user-facing workflow — owner/admin, succession, invitation — described in the meeting conclusion this revision is based on) and R-02 (two Figma elements accepted-in-principle but never drawn). I looked for but did not find additional gaps of comparable weight in: rename/settings behind `[設定]` (plausible minor gap but no concrete decision text points at it being expected this round, unlike `[成員]`), a way back from a system/diagram context to its owning project (covered by the breadcrumb), or an indication of what happens to a project's data when it has zero systems yet (covered by the empty state in §15).

### Summary

Two Major findings (R-01, R-02) trace to the same root cause: this revision inherited real new obligations from the meeting conclusion and the Figma read, and it consistently *named* the ones it chose not to act on (`[F5]=C`'s deferred alternatives, Flow 7's non-dependency, the clear/delete gap) but silently under-delivered on two others — the `[成員]` button and the two "視為新增" Figma elements. The pattern the project's own corrections log repeatedly calls out — "揭露承諾與實際產出間的落差" — recurs here in a new form: things that were *decided* to be added were not *drawn*. R-03 is a lower-confidence Major flagging a real downstream risk (capability 11's tab split masking three different verification surfaces) that this stage is not required to resolve but should flag so `units-generation` doesn't default to treating it as one unit. Nothing found under attack points 2 and 4 — those two pieces of reasoning held up under direct verification against the app source and the upstream scope decisions.
